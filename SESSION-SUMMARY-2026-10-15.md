## §6 doctrine: Option II (captain-retract, no stain)

The recommended §6 retraction doctrine is Option II. The load-bearing invariant any option must pass: the retract-then-supersede cycle test. HANDOFF.md §3 updated to list this as a captain-call item. The 3 options:

- **I: pure self-retraction** — the author of the original task can retract it; no captain involvement.
- **II: captain-retract, no stain** — only a captain can retract a task; the original task is removed but the descendants are NOT marked as `stained`.
- **III: captain-retract, AND stain** — only a captain can retract; descendants ARE marked as `stained` so anyone reading the lineage knows they came from a retracted record.

The 1b design duel is referenced everywhere. Casey implements the brief-specified effect-voiding model on top of GLM's design.
